How to make Lasagna

Ingredients

  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese (divided)
  • 1 large egg
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • ¼ cup water
  • 24 oz (1 jar) high-quality marinara sauce
  • 1 lb (450g) ground beef or Italian sausage

Steps

  1. Brown the Mea
  2. Mix the Cheese: In a small bowl, stir together the ricotta, egg, and 1 cup of the mozzarella.
  3. Assemble: In an 8x12 or 9x13-inch baking dis
  4. Bake: Cover tightly with aluminum foil. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 10 minutes until bubbly and golden
  5. Rest: Let it rest for 10 minutes before slicing so it holds its shape.
